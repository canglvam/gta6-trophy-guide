Do this first — the one trophy you can miss.

Positive Mental Attitude: after the Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, find Lucia in the kitchen at Jason’s house in the morning. She mentions meditating and walks to the balcony — follow her and accept. That balcony meditation may be required for the trophy and it is gone after Chapter 1. The other 18 spots are covered in the guide below.

Roadmap to the Platinum

Seven stages, in order. Story first, then strangers, activities, collectibles and crime — 100% completion last. About 200 hours all in; the story alone is 60–80.

    Where the guides come from

    Every guide is written in our own words for this page.
