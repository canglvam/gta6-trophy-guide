Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough is enough — there is no mission replay, but free roam carries on after the story, and all 55 trophies are offline and single-player. Work the stages in order; the 100% checklist (stage 7) only opens up after the story. Expect around 200 hours for the Platinum as a whole. Sources: GTABase, KeenGamer, Push Square.

Watch out — the one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) is the only trophy GTABase and PowerPyx flag as possibly missable. Do not skip Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, and meditate at each spot as you find it.

    About this guide

    Names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles — the two agree on every name, description and grade. 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned. Guides are written in our own words for this entry and cite their sources beside each trophy; nothing is copied from another guide. Where no guide has confirmed a method yet, the guide says so instead of guessing.

    Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your machine. Export downloads a JSON file you can import back later. The app fetches nothing over the network — it works with the network off.
