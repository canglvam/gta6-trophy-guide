      About this guide

      Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list as published for Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned). Every guide is written for this page in our own words and names its sources beside it — a source is cited, never copied. Counts and places come from the guides cited under each trophy. Where the guides still disagree this soon after launch, the card says so instead of guessing.
